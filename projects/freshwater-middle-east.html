Research project Research I Led

Freshwater Biodiversity in the Arid Landscapes of the Middle East

2023–2025

This project addresses major freshwater biodiversity data gaps in the Middle East through the development of the first georeferenced database of Odonata occurrences across the region, alongside a complementary functional-trait database. Using these datasets, it investigates how water availability and land use shape taxonomic, functional, and phylogenetic diversity, with particular attention to the potential conservation value of irrigation-associated water bodies in regions where natural freshwater habitats are scarce.

Institution

German Centre for Integrative Biodiversity Research (iDiv), Leipzig, Germany

Methods & Skills

R programming · Large-scale biodiversity data integration and harmonization · Functional trait and phylogenetic data compilation · Spatial and environmental data analysis · Taxonomic, functional, and phylogenetic diversity analysis · High-Performance Computing (HPC) environments

Related Publication

  • Barzoki, Z. E. & Chase, J. (2026). Water availability and land-use shape the diversity of Odonata in the Middle East. Insect Conservation and Diversity.
