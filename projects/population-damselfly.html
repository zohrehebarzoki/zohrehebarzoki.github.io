Research project Research I Led

Population Ecology and Spatial Habitat Use in a Polymorphic Damselfly

2022

This project investigated the population ecology of Ischnura elegans in an arid region of Central Iran. Using individual marking and capture–recapture methods, it examined differences in survival, detection probability, population density, and space use between sexes and female colour morphs. The findings revealed behavioural and spatial patterns that may reflect how damselfly populations respond to the challenges of living in arid environments.

Institution

Shiraz University, Iran

Methods & Skills

Capture–mark–recapture field studies · Individual-based population monitoring · Cormack–Jolly–Seber survival modelling · Spatial capture–recapture modelling · Population density estimation and spatial prediction

Related Publication
