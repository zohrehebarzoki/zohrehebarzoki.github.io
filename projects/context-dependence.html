Research project Research I Led

Context Dependence in Biodiversity–Environment Relationships Across Contrasting Climatic Regions

2026

This project examines biodiversity–environment relationships across contrasting climatic regions with varying levels of biodiversity data availability. The findings reveal context-dependent responses in taxonomic and functional diversity, highlighting the limitations of extrapolating ecological patterns from well-studied to understudied regions.

Institution

German Centre for Integrative Biodiversity Research (iDiv), Leipzig, Germany

Methods & Skills

R programming · Large-scale species occurrence data integration and harmonization · Functional trait data compilation · Land-cover and climate data processing · Taxonomic and functional diversity analysis
