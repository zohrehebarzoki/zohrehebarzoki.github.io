Research project Collaborative Research

Bird Diversity and Community Structure Across Environmental Gradients in Iran

2025–2026

This project investigates patterns of bird diversity in Iran. By examining multiple dimensions of biodiversity, including functional and phylogenetic diversity, it explores how bird community composition and diversity vary along environmental gradients and across degraded wetland ecosystems. The research aims to improve our understanding of bird biodiversity patterns and inform conservation priorities in these vulnerable landscapes.

Institution

Shiraz University, Iran

Role

Statistical analysis of bird diversity and community structure

Methods & Skills

Functional and phylogenetic diversity analysis · Environmental gradient analysis · Community composition analysis
