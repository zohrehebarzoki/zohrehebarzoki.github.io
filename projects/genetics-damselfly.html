Research project Research I Led

Genetic Diversity and Population Structure of Damselflies

2019

This project investigated the genetic diversity and population structure of Ischnura elegans in the arid landscapes of central Iran. Conducted during a research visit to Lund University, Sweden, it examined genetic variation within and among populations to better understand patterns of population differentiation in water-limited environments.

Institution

Lund University, Sweden

Methods & Skills

DNA extraction · RAD-seq library preparation · NGS data processing · Genetic diversity and population structure analysis
