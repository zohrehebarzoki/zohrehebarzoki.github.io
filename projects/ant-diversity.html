Research project Collaborative Research

Environmental Drivers of Ant Diversity in Arid Landscapes

2023

This project investigated patterns of ant diversity and community composition along an elevational gradient in the arid landscapes of Central Iran. By examining the influence of climate, soil characteristics, and geographical distance, it explored how environmental conditions shape biodiversity patterns across contrasting habitats.

Institution

Shiraz University, Iran

Role

Statistical analysis of ant diversity and community structure · Manuscript preparation

Methods & Skills

Taxonomic diversity analysis · Generalized linear mixed modelling · Community composition analysis

Related Publication

  • Hajian, M., Sadeghi, S., Eslami Barzoki, Z., Moradmand, M., Gholamhosseini, A. & Ebrahimi, M. (2024). Ant diversity and species assemblages along an elevational gradient in the arid area of Central Iran. Journal of Insect Biodiversity and Systematics.
